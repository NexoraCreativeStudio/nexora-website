# Nexora — Client payment communication templates

Tone: **professional, calm, clear, non-threatening.** No legal threats, no aggressive suspension language, no invented terms. For any service-review/pause step, follow the commercial agreement and legal terms — not an email.

Placeholders in `[BRACKETS]`.

---

## 1. Invoice issued

> Subject: Your invoice [NX-INV-…] is ready
>
> Hi [First name],
>
> Your invoice **[NX-INV-…]** is ready. It covers **[service / milestone]** and is due on **[due date]**.
>
> **[Payment instructions — bank details / payment link, as activated]**
>
> If anything doesn't look right, reply to this email and we'll sort it out before payment.
>
> Thanks,
> The Nexora team
